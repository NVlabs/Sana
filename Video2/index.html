SANA-Video 2.0

Hybrid Linear Attention with Attention Residuals for Efficient Video Generation

Junsong Chen, Jincheng Yu, Yitong Li, Shuchen Xue, Haozhe Liu, Jingyu Xin, Yuyang Zhao, Zhangjie Wu, Zian Wang, Daquan Zhou, Ping Luo, Song Han, Enze Xie

NVIDIA

Paper ↗ Code ↗

Abstract

We introduce SANA-Video 2.0, a hybrid video diffusion transformer instantiated at 5B and 14B scales under one unified architecture. The design generates high-quality video up to 720p on a single GPU, matching strong full-softmax Video DiTs in quality while retaining the favorable long-sequence scaling of linear attention. Hybrid Linear–Softmax Attention combines gated linear attention with periodic gated-softmax anchors, while Block Attention Residuals propagate refreshed representations across depth. With 40-step sampling, SANA-Video 2.0 reaches VBench Total 84.30 in 13.2 seconds at 480p on one H100. Its compiled DiT forward is 3.2× faster than matched full softmax at a 720p/60s shape. Full-stack Sol-Engine optimization further accelerates the backbone by 3.58×, bringing the 5B pipeline to 13.06s at 720p/5s and making it 120× faster than Wan 2.2-A14B on one H100.
