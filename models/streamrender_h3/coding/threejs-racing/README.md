# CodeGameEngine — TORCS Geometry Edition

A Three.js racing engine with 14-class semantic-stream export. This version
uses the TORCS E-Track 3 circuit, the car1-stock1 vehicle model, and their
original textures. Track elevation, road boundaries, and vehicle silhouettes
come from the original geometry. Vehicles are controlled by this project's
arcade physics and autopilot; semantic frames are generated by the engine
rather than replayed or copied from the training dataset.

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:5191. Drive with W/A/S/D or the arrow keys, use Space
for the handbrake, and press R to reset. The page provides autopilot,
recording, and live semantic-stream controls. Recordings are saved under
`exports/`; the default is 719 frames at 24 fps and 1344 × 768 resolution,
approximately 30 seconds.

- `public/palette.json`: the 14-class RGB palette matching the TORCS data.
- `src/native-assets.js`: original asset loading and semantic materials.
  Transparent textures use binary alpha cutouts; semantic output uses no
  color blending or antialiasing.
- `src/native-world.js`: original road-height sampling and the closed-loop
  road centerline.
- `src/main.js`: driving, camera control, opponent vehicles, and per-frame
  export.

The original assets come from https://github.com/jzbontar/torcs:
`data/tracks/road/e-track-3` and `data/cars/models/car1-stock1`. Models are
converted to JSON, and SGI RGB textures are converted to PNG. The track
authors are Eric Espie and Bernhard Wymann; the vehicle author is Bernhard
Wymann. Art assets and their format conversions follow the
[Free Art License](https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/). Original notices
are preserved in `licenses/`. Preserve the source-code and art-asset
licenses separately.

See the [StreamRender-H3 runtime README](../../README.md) for online H3
render configuration, the weight manifest, and launch instructions. Clicking
the semantic-stream button connects the frontend to the resident GPU worker
through the `/runtime` proxy. Offline recording and export remain available.
