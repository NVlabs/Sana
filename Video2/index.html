SANA-Video 2.0

Hybrid Linear Attention with Attention Residuals for Efficient Video Generation

Abstract

We introduce SANA-Video 2.0, a mostly-linear video diffusion transformer instantiated at 5B and 14B scales under one shared architecture. The design generates high-quality video up to 720p on a single GPU, matching strong full-softmax Video DiTs in quality while retaining the favorable long-sequence scaling of linear attention. To avoid quadratic attention throughout, Hybrid Linear–Softmax Attention combines gated linear attention for O(N)-dominated mixing with periodic gated-softmax anchors at a regular 3:1 ratio (25% softmax), restoring the full-rank token interactions that pure linear attention cannot express. To propagate these refreshed representations across depth, Block Attention Residuals route completed block summaries into later linear layers, enabling anchor-feature reuse and increasing deep-layer effective rank by ~12%. Through from-scratch training, we learn the complete hybrid from random initialization, and reduced-resolution proxy studies identify 25% softmax as the quality–efficiency knee. With 40-step sampling, SANA-Video 2.0 reaches VBench Total 84.30 in 13.2 seconds at 480×832×81 on one H100. Its compiled DiT forward is 3.2× faster than matched full softmax at a 720p/60s shape, an advantage that widens with duration. Sol-Engine further accelerates this hardware-friendly backbone by 3.58× end to end on B200. Overall, the mostly-linear hybrid recovers softmax-level expressiveness at substantially lower cost and scales to long, high-resolution video.
